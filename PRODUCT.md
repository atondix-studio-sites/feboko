# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

German and European business leaders evaluating, entering, or operating in the Indian market. They need an experienced cross-border partner who can reduce regulatory, administrative, financial, legal, and operational uncertainty from market entry through ongoing operations.

## Product Purpose

Present FeBoKo Consulting as the practical bridge between European companies and successful operations in India. The site should build confidence in FeBoKo's local expertise, explain its full-service offer, introduce its specialists, and convert qualified visitors into initial-consultation enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Operational, experienced, trustworthy. The tone is direct and professional, with international perspective and concrete local competence rather than abstract consultancy language.

## Anti-references

Do not drift from the established feboko.com identity or turn the site into a generic SaaS landing page, a flashy startup site, or an ornamental editorial layout. Avoid invented colors, typefaces, card systems, and motion that are absent from the original WordPress theme. Do not substitute visual novelty for rendered parity with the production site.

## Design Principles

- Match the production identity before improving anything.
- Show operational capability through specific services, people, and real market context.
- Keep calls to action clear without making the experience feel sales-heavy.
- Preserve the bilingual German/English experience and equivalent interaction behavior.
- Treat responsive and keyboard behavior as part of visual fidelity, not as optional polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific conformance level is documented. Preserve and improve the original site's semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, labelled controls, meaningful image alternatives, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support without changing its visual identity.
